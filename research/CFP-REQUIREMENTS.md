## The two clauses that bind us

1. **Unique content.** You own the IP, and the title and recording must differ from anything already used. **Automation Guild gets the first conference outing.**
2. **Record on the TestGuild deck.** Selected speakers are sent the template. Our deck is therefore the content and design master, not the final file.

## Topics they want

Agentic testing and AI agents in the SDLC · evals and "LLM as a judge" · test strategy when developers use AI · Playwright in real pipelines (MCP vs CLI cost) · automation that survives (flakes, self-heal that still fails) · test data and environments · API under faster delivery · career and test leadership · honest tool changes · performance · security/DevSecOps · **mobile, only as a real case** · continuous testing as the setting for flakes and agents.

## Focus on

- A real team, a real constraint, **a number you will stand behind**. Case studies ranked 151, deep-dive demos 140, workshops 121.
- Agentic work: what the agent may do, when a human stops it, how you judge the output.
- **Evals / LLM as a judge** — "so a green suite is not *the implementation asserting itself*."
- People in the trenches, named practitioners — not SMEs representing a tool.
- **Materials after the talk: repo, slides, the exact command they watched.**

## Avoid

- Product tours and UI walkthroughs. **"If the logo is required for the talk to make sense, it is a pitch. Rewrite it."** They already have 247 of these.
- Theoretical AI overviews, trend roundups, "the future of testing" with no repo and no scar.
- An all-AI generator grid. One generation story is enough.
- Accessibility, desktop/embedded, cloud blobs, "basics revisited" — unless you have a specific trench case.
- High-level sponsor keynotes; panels of vendors.

## Why our angle is what it is

A straight "here is my new mobile automation tool" session is the exact shape the avoid-list describes, and the electorate is the community. The material that wins is the same material reframed: **the tool is the setting, the verification failures are the content.** Their top ask — a green suite that is not the implementation asserting itself — is our defect record almost verbatim.
