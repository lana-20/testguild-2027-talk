Hi, I'm Lana Begunova. I'm an SDET in Seattle, and I build Mobium, an open-source tool for automating mobile apps.

My talk is "Fast and Robust Mobile Tests with Mobium."

Here's the idea. Animations are for humans. To a test, every slide-in and fade is either time spent waiting, or a race it can lose — a slow suite, or a flaky one.

The standard advice is to turn animations off. I measured what that actually does, on a real iPhone, a real Pixel, a simulator and an emulator. The system switch reaches an app's own animations only when the app listens. So I'll show you how to make it listen — one line per framework — how to prove the motion is really gone, with a control, and what to do with the motion you can't turn off.

With the motion off, the same test steps took a quarter to nearly half less time on every device. Everything runs from one test file, through Mobium's command line and MCP — what an AI agent uses.

See you at the Guild.
