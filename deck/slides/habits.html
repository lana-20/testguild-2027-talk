Four habits for fast, durable mobile tests

01

Turn off the system's animations once per device, and put them back as you found them.

02

Make the app honor Reduce Motion. The fast path becomes a real path, in the build you ship.

03

Time it with the app, and keep a control. A setting that reads 0 is not an animation that stopped.

04

Wait for stillness; refuse what never stops. A chased target is a flake waiting to happen.
