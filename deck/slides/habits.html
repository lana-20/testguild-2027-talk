Four habits. All cheap. All earned the hard way.

01

Give every negative result a positive control. Show it a thing it must catch, before you trust a zero.

02

Read the state back. Capture both streams. There is no convention about which one carries the error.

03

Mutate, then watch it fail. A test you have never seen fail is a test you have not tested.

04

Capture fixtures from the real thing. When you fix a heuristic, keep a capture that fails under the old rule.
