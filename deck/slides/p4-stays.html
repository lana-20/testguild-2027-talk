Some motion never stops. Wait for stillness — or refuse.

$ mobium tap @e2   # a falling piece of confetti

error: failed check stable: it is still moving after 5s —

it was at [297,1260][315,1293] and is now at [306,1530][327,1560]

$ echo $?

6   # timeout

Spinners, confetti, live content. Mobium waits for every target to stop moving before it acts — and a target that never holds still is a failure with a code, not something to chase.

Chasing a moving target is how a race becomes a flake: sometimes the tap lands, sometimes it does not, and the retry hides it. A refusal fails the same way every time.
