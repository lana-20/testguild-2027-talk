Three costs, and the first one is measurable

costwhat happenswhat it costs you
timethe tool waits for the target to settle~2 s a sliding target — measured
racesa tap aimed at something still moving; a read mid-transitiona flake that a retry hides
CPUthe device under test renders frames nobody watchesa busier device

Two seconds is invisible in one test. Across a suite, on every run, for everyone waiting on the build, it is the feedback loop.

Two seconds: a target that slides in over two seconds cost 3.3 s on the iPhone and 2.5 s on the Android emulator when the tool waited for it, against 1.3 s and 0.3 s with the motion off — the demo test through the CLI, medians of three. The race cost does not show up as time — it shows up as a retry.
