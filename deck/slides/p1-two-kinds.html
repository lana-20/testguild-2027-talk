Two kinds of animation. Different switches.

The system's

Transitions between screens and apps, drawn by the OS.

A device setting turns them off.

The app's own

Anything the app animates itself: slide-ins, bounces, confetti.

Only the app can turn them off.

This distinction carries the rest of the talk. Part 2 is the left box, Part 3 the right one — and Part 3 is where the time actually goes.
