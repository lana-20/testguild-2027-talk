Rule 01

Any result of “zero” or “no difference” needs a positive control.

Make the measurement report the thing you expect it to find. Then believe the zero.

The probe now asserts that the hierarchy names the app it asked for, before it believes any count at all.

A probe that never ran and a check that silently passes are the same defect in different clothes.

First of the four habits. Say the number of the rule — the audience is collecting them, and you will show all four again at the end.
