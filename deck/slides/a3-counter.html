The honest counter-example

One feature went to both platforms, and neither found anything.

In a defect table that is otherwise a column of yes, that is notable rather than reassuring — so it is written down as the exception, with the reason it is plausible.

A record that only contains the times you were wrong is not a record. It is a performance.

If you keep a log like this, keep the clean results in it too. They are how you tell a real finding from a story you enjoy telling.

This slide buys you credibility for everything else. It also pre-empts the sceptic in the Q&A who assumes you cherry-picked.
