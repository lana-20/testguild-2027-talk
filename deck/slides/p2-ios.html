iOS: no command. Reduce Motion, in Settings.

Settings › Accessibility › Motion › Reduce Motion

Nothing outside the phone can set it. A test that wants it has to drive the Settings app — about 25 seconds a change on a real iPhone, measured.

So: once per device, in setup. Never per test.

Reduce Motion reduces; it does not remove. Settings' own push still slides with it on. Its real power is what it tells the app — which is Part 3.
