With Mobium

One call, both platforms, read back, put back.

mobium accessibility reduce_motion on

The three scales on Android; a trip through Settings on an iPhone. Confirmed by reading the setting back, and returned to what it was when the session ends.

A setting changed on somebody's phone and never changed back is a defect, whoever wrote the test. So the demo's recordings read it before and after.

Droppable if tight: its point returns on the habits card. The restore matters most on a real phone — the iPhone in the demo is the speaker's own.
