Off means each platform's own switch: Reduce Motion on iOS, all three scales at 0 on Android, set by the test's first step. The control holds on every device. With animations on the case also waits out the falling confetti, which is animation too. On Android the honoring time with the motion off lands at about 0.25 or about 1.1 s, in both clients — that is how long Mobium's tap takes to confirm the target is still — so a median of three can fall on either; the MCP and CLI rows differ there for that reason, not because of the client.
